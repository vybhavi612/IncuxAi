01Overview

EWM (Enterprise Work Management) is a centralized digital workplace platform built for modern enterprises. It brings task tracking, meetings, and team collaboration into a single, secure environment, replacing fragmented point tools with one unified system of record for organizational work.

This documentation is written for three audiences: administrators setting up an organization, end users doing their day-to-day work, and IT or security stakeholders evaluating the platform ahead of rollout.

Note

This documentation reflects the publicly described feature set of EWM as of July 2026. Screens, configuration options, and terminology may evolve between releases.

02Key Platform Capabilities

EWM is organized around four core pillars of enterprise work management.

Intuitive Dashboard

A bird's-eye view of the entire organization's workflow at a glance — built for fast status reviews without digging into individual projects.

Team Collaboration

Assign tasks, track progress, and keep every stakeholder aligned on what's next and who owns it, without leaving the platform.

Smart Scheduling

Built-in calendar and meeting integrations keep everyone on the same page, next to the work itself rather than in a separate app.

Enterprise Security

Bank-grade encryption and role-based access control, so administrators can govern exactly who sees and does what.

Dashboard & visibility

The dashboard is the primary landing surface after sign-in. It surfaces organization-wide workflow status without requiring users to navigate into individual projects, supporting faster reporting and status reviews.

Collaboration & task management

EWM centers on shared task ownership. Teams can:

  • Assign tasks to individuals or teams
  • Track progress against assigned work
  • Collaborate directly within the platform rather than across disconnected channels

Scheduling & meetings

Calendar and meeting integrations are built in, so scheduling happens alongside task and project work instead of in a separate application.

Security & access control

Encryption and role-based access control (RBAC) work together so organizations can define exactly what different roles are permitted to see and do inside the platform. See the Security section below for rollout considerations.

03Getting Started

Create an account

  1. 1Go to the EWM homepage.
  2. 2Select Get Started or Create an account.
  3. 3Complete the sign-up flow to provision an account.
  4. 4Sign in to land on the main Dashboard.

Sign in

Existing users can sign in directly from Sign In on the homepage, or via Go to Dashboard — both route to the login screen.

First steps after login

  • Review the Dashboard for an overview of current organizational workflow
  • Set up or join the teams relevant to your role
  • Create or review tasks assigned to you
  • Connect your calendar to take advantage of smart scheduling

04Security Considerations

Enterprise adoption of any work management platform typically needs to align with internal IT and security policy. Before a broad rollout, walk through the following.

Encryption

Confirm how data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and how that aligns with internal data-protection requirements.

Access control

Define role-based access policies before rollout, mapping organizational roles to platform permissions.

Identity management

Decide whether EWM will integrate with your existing identity provider or SSO for centralized user lifecycle management.

Data governance

Establish retention, export, and audit practices consistent with company policy and applicable regulation.

Recommendation

Organizations with formal compliance requirements (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, etc.) should request detailed security and compliance documentation directly from the EWM vendor before enterprise-wide deployment.
